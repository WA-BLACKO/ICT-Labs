THE IDEA

Why I Built ICT Lab

The Idea

ICT contains concepts that become much easier when students can actually interact with them. ICT Lab was created to bring those concepts to life.

For Students

The project combines learning resources, calculators, simulations, coding tools, quizzes and other utilities in one place for A/L ICT students.

The Ambition

My goal is to continue developing ICT Lab into a smarter learning platform with more interactive tools, better simulations and intelligent learning assistance.
